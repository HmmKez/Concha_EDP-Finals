HOW TO PLAY
▶
Start the Game
Press the START button or hit Enter. The one-minute timer video begins immediately — there is no pause.
⌨
Type the Text
Type the displayed passage exactly as shown. Green = correct. Red = wrong. Every character matters.
⚡
Race the Timer
Watch both progress bars. If the video bar reaches 100% before your typing bar does — you die.
💀
Instant Death
When the video ends, the game ends. No extensions, no second chances. The clock shows no mercy.
🏆
Win Condition
Finish typing the full passage before the video ends. Your WPM and accuracy are recorded on the results screen.
🎯
Difficulty Levels
Easy = short passage. Medium = longer. Hard = a paragraph of pure suffering. All within the same 60-second video.
